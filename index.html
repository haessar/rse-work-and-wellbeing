About the study

We are surveying Research Software Engineers working in the UK to better understand how neurodivergence, mental health and working arrangements relate to wellbeing, support and productivity.

The study follows work by InclusiveRSE exploring the experiences of RSEs from underrepresented groups, alongside work such as the SSI Fellowship project ConveRSE highlighting mental health and wellbeing within the research software community.

What will we explore?

Quantitative

Identify patterns

Working arrangements, wellbeing and workplace support.

Qualitative

Understand experiences

Strategies and adjustments that support wellbeing and motivation.
